Why the Doppler decides so much

Vein symptoms come from one of two places.

Leak

Valves in the superficial veins of the leg fail and blood runs backwards. By far the commonest cause. Shows clearly on a Doppler. Simple to fix.

Block

A narrowing or blockage in the deep veins inside the abdomen — the iliac veins. Also fixable, but a bigger, costlier and more technical procedure.

So the leak is looked for first, on the Doppler. The block is considered only when symptoms are advanced and the superficial Doppler shows nothing significant.
